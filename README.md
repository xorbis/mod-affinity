# mod-affinity

An [AzerothCore](https://www.azerothcore.org/) module: at level 60 a character discovers its **affinity**, one of the class skills it knows, drawn at random and doubled for the rest of its life.

> Xorbis has reached level 60 and discovered his affinity for [Fireball].

The choice is announced to everyone online and in the character's guild chat, and the boosted skill's own tooltip shows the doubled numbers, on every rank, without a client patch.

## How it works

Each candidate skill has a hidden passive (a `spell_dbc` row, ids 250001+) carrying a **+100 % spell modifier** on the skill's family bits: `SPELL_AURA_ADD_PCT_MODIFIER` with op 0 (`SPELLMOD_DAMAGE`) for direct damage and heals, op 22 (`SPELLMOD_DOT`) for periodic ticks, op 8 (`SPELLMOD_ALL_EFFECTS`) for buffs and absorbs. That is exactly how the *Improved <skill>* talents are built (Improved Eviscerate, Improved Corruption, Improved Power Word: Fortitude), so the core applies it in the same places (`Unit::SpellDamageBonusDone`, `SpellHealingBonusDone`, `MeleeDamageBonusDone`, `SpellEffectInfo::CalcValue`) and the client, which receives the modifier like any talent's, draws the doubled values into the tooltips. Damage and heals double after spell power; buffs double their base value; absorbs double their base (the spell-power part of a shield is added by a script afterwards and is not doubled).

The passive is applied at discovery and put back at every login (passives are not saved with a character's auras); the choice lives in `character_affinity`. Talent-granted skills are deliberately not in the pool: a respec would strand the affinity.

`affinity_pool` (world database) holds the 129 candidates: 13 warrior, 11 paladin, 11 hunter, 9 rogue, 15 priest, 9 death knight, 15 shaman, 15 mage, 13 warlock, 18 druid — damage, heals, DoTs/HoTs and buffs, trainer-taught (or quest-taught for death knights) at level 60 or below. Its `weight` column tunes the roll (`0` takes an entry out; `.affinity reload` picks changes up), the roll only considers skills the character actually knows. The table and the `spell_dbc` rows are generated by `tools/gen_affinity.py` from the pool defined at the top of that script and the client's `Spell.dbc`; the script refuses two entries of one class whose masks overlap and prints what else every mask touches, so a pool change is reviewed before it ships.

## Bots

With [mod-playerbots](https://github.com/mod-playerbots/mod-playerbots), the realm's random bots (the `rndbot` accounts and the `.playerbots bot addclass` bots) get nothing unless `Affinity.RandomBots = 1`: they are recycled constantly and every 60+ bot would announce itself at each realm start. A player's own characters driven by the bot AI are players: they roll at 60, are announced, and show their entry when their owner logs them in.

## Client addon

Everything else the player sees is drawn by the realm's addon (`XorWoW`, this repository's `client\Interface\AddOns\XorWoW\Affinity.lua`): an **Affinity** entry in the spellbook, in the tab of the boosted skill and with its icon; an *Affinity: damage doubled* line on the boosted skill's tooltip; a banner with the level-up chime when the affinity is discovered. The module tells the addon what was rolled through an addon message (prefix `XorWoW`, `AFFINITY;NEW|CUR;<pool id>;<spell id>;<text>;<skill names>`) at login and at discovery, and answers the addon's `AFFINITY?` request after a UI reload. Without the addon the doubling, the doubled tooltips and the announcements still work; only the drawn extras are missing.

## Commands

| Command | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `.affinity [name]` | everyone | Your affinity, or someone else's. |
| `.affinity reroll [name]` | GM | Roll again (announced). |
| `.affinity set <pool id> [name]` | GM | Give a specific pool entry (not announced). |
| `.affinity reset [name]` | GM | Remove it; the next level-up or login rolls a new one. |
| `.affinity list [class id]` | GM, console | The pool. |
| `.affinity reload` | admin, console | Reload `affinity_pool`. |

## Install

1. Clone into `modules/mod-affinity` and rebuild the core. The SQL under `data/sql` is applied by the database updater (`db-world`: pool + passives, `db-characters`: `character_affinity`).
2. `conf/mod_affinity.conf.dist` documents the settings: `Affinity.Enable`, `Affinity.Level` (60), `Affinity.Announce`, `Affinity.AnnounceGuild`, `Affinity.RandomBots`.
3. Characters already past the level roll at their next login.

To change the pool, edit `POOL` in `tools/gen_affinity.py`, run it against a 3.3.5a `Spell.dbc` (`docker cp ac-worldserver:/azerothcore/env/dist/data/dbc/Spell.dbc .`), review its report, commit the regenerated SQL. Pool ids are permanent: append, never renumber.
